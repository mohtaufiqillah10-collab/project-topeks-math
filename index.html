Modeling Kedah-Kelantan (KK) Cattle Weight Gain

An interactive application demonstrating how derivatives ($f'(x)$) optimize feeding regimes and predict peak growth efficiency in local Malaysian beef cattle.

The Bioscience Scenario

In Malaysian animal husbandry, understanding the growth trajectory of the native Kedah-Kelantan (KK) cattle is vital for precision management. Growth typically follows a sigmoidal path.

By modeling weight $f(x)$ over time (weeks), we use the first derivative $f'(x)$ to find the exact moment of maximum growth velocity. This helps farmers transition cattle from high-protein starter diets to finish feeds cost-effectively.

Growth Function $f(x)$
$$f(x) = \frac{280}{1 + 9e^{-0.08x}}$$

Yields total body weight in kilograms (kg) at week $x$.

Derivative Function $f'(x)$
$$f'(x) = \frac{201.6 \cdot e^{-0.08x}}{(1 + 9e^{-0.08x})^2}$$

Yields current growth rate in kilograms per week ($\text{kg/week}$) at week $x$.

Data & Baseline Reference: Based on sigmoidal growth curves compiled by the Malaysian Agricultural Research and Development Institute (MARDI) and historical datasets from the Department of Veterinary Services (DVS) Malaysia.
